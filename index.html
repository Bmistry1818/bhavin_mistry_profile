How I think about the work

A useful idea
is only the start.

01

Start with the problem.

Choose a use case with a clear business purpose. Define what a better outcome would look like before choosing the technology.

02

Build for the real world.

Bring engineering, product and architecture together. Think about adoption, reliability and the people who will use the system.

03

Make trust part of delivery.

In financial services, governance and risk belong in the design from the beginning. Keep the path from experiment to production practical.
