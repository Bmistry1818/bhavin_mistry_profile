Strategic Thesis

Why Most Enterprise AI Transformations Stall

Most enterprise AI and platform transformations fail not because the foundation models are weak, but because the engineering operating model, governance perimeter, and unit economics were never designed for production scale.

Bhavin Mistry is an Enterprise AI and Engineering Executive who bridges C-suite commercial strategy with deep systems execution. Across his leadership tenure at Commonwealth Bank, and previously at Acenda, Accenture, and Deloitte, he has built and led high-performing engineering teams that modernize legacy cores, deliver cloud-native platforms, and transition Generative and Agentic AI into strictly governed enterprise production.

With a rare academic triad spanning Corporate Finance & Commerce (B.Com), Advanced Computer Applications (MCA), and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (The University of Texas at Austin), Bhavin operates fluently across both the architecture review board and the executive P&L table—ensuring every engineering roadmap compounds both technical velocity and commercial return.
